Questions traders ask

Is SwipeTrainer free?

Yes. It's free to download and play on iPhone and Android, supported by ads.

Is this a game to learn stock trading?

Exactly. SwipeTrainer is a game built to teach chart reading. You swipe bullish or bearish on real historical stock and crypto charts, then see what actually happened and why, building the pattern recognition that trading depends on.

Do I need trading experience?

Not at all. A guided intro teaches the core patterns in under two minutes, and the difficulty scales from beginner to advanced as you improve.

How do I practice reading candlestick charts?

SwipeTrainer gives you hundreds of real candlestick setups to call, with instant feedback and the key signal explained on every one. That repetition is the fastest way to internalize what candlestick patterns are telling you.

Is it good for swing trading?

Yes. Setups span breakouts, breakdowns, flags, double tops and more over multi-day horizons. These are the exact patterns swing traders watch for.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. SwipeTrainer is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

Does SwipeTrainer give financial advice?

No. SwipeTrainer is an educational training game using historical market data. It is not financial advice and does not offer real trading. Always do your own research.
