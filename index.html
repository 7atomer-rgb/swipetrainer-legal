Common questions

Is this real trading?
No. SwipeTrainer is an educational game. Every chart is historical market data, there is no real money and nothing in the app is investment advice.

Do I need an account?
No. The whole app works without signing in. An account (Sign in with Apple or Google) only keeps your stats across devices.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also see the details on our account deletion page, or email us and we will do it for you.

How do I reset my stats?
In the app: Settings → Reset stats.

Charts or ads are not loading.
Check your internet connection and reopen the app. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.
