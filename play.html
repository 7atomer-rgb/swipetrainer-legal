Free stock chart quiz: bullish or bearish?

Five real historical charts. Call each one, see what happened over the next 10 trading days, and learn the pattern behind it. No sign-up.

How it works

  1. You see a real stock chart up to one day. The date and the future are hidden.
  2. Tap Bullish or Bearish (or swipe, or use the arrow keys).
  3. The next 10 trading days play out, and you see the pattern and the lesson.

The charts are real, taken from our library of hand-picked setups that follow strict textbook rules for each of 8 chart patterns. Some of them fail on purpose, because real patterns do.
